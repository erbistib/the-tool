    Method. Market cap = latest price × shares outstanding today (sheet count, else the model's basic count). Forward cap for year t = cap − dividends paid from this year through t−1. EV = cap + net debt(t). Multiples use the model's EBITDA, net income, FCFE, dividends and NAV for year t, in model currency. Targets and NAV/share are converted at sheet FX. Sector lines are medians of positive values. Click a multiple or financial to chart it through the forecast; click a price for the history.
